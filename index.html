Quillmark Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

F. A. GENERAL SERVICES LLC ("we", "us") makes Quillmark. This page says what the app does with your information. The short version is that it keeps everything on your phone and sends none of it to us.

The short version

We do not collect your personal information. There is no account to create, no server that receives your bills, and no analytics, advertising or tracking in the app.

What the app does with your data

Your data stays on your phone

Quillmark has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your bills, balances and payment history are stored on this phone, not on any server of ours. The app does not send them anywhere, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What can leave your phone

Only what you choose to send out: a backup you copy or save, or a calendar file you export, which go wherever you put them. Buying Pro goes through the App Store or Google Play, which handle your payment; the app never sees your card or your Apple or Google account details.

Your phone's own backup

If your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) is switched on, it may include the app's data. That is a setting on your phone, not something the app does, and we never receive it. Saved details are kept out of it where the phone allows: on iPhone they are excluded, and on Android they are encrypted with a key that never leaves the phone, so a copy elsewhere cannot be read.

Reminders and the lock

Reminders are scheduled on your phone and fire without an internet connection. Saved details are kept in your phone's secure storage behind your screen lock. The app never sees your fingerprint or face, only the phone's yes or no, and saved details are never put into a backup made with the app.

Deleting your data

Everything the app holds is on this phone, so deleting the app deletes your bills and payments. Saved details kept in secure storage may remain on some phones after the app is deleted, so remove them from inside the app first if that matters to you.

Third parties

The only outside service involved is the store you download the app from. Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ and https://policies.google.com/privacy). We receive no personal information from them beyond what the store needs to confirm that a purchase was made.

Children

Quillmark is a tool for managing bills and debts and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Because we hold none of your personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. Your data is on your phone, and you can export it with the backup feature or delete it by removing the app. If you have a question about this policy, write to us.

Changes to this policy

If what the app does with data ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches you.

Not financial advice

Quillmark is a calculator and a reminder. It is not a bank, not a bill payer, and not a financial adviser. Every payment remains yours to make.
